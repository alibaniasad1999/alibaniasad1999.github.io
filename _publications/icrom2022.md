title: "Linear Quadratic Integral Differential Game applied to the Real-time Control of a Quadrotor Experimental setup"
authors: "Hadi Nobahari, Ali Baniasad, Alireza Sharifi"
conference: "2022 10th RSI International Conference on Robotics and Mechatronics (ICRoM)"
year: 2022
redirect: "https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/document/10025263"
---

A linear quadratic differential game with integral action (LQIDG) is implemented in real time on a 3-DoF quadrotor stand. The two-player Nash-equilibrium formulation provides robust tracking of Euler-angle commands under worst-case disturbances, validated on physical hardware.
